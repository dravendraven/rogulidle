Observed ruler — two probes, no model

Challenge and reward measured by actually clearing a floor twice — once picking up nothing, once picking up whatever is in the way — instead of summing a duel formula over the roster. See src/analysis/observed-ruler.js and docs/curve-shape.md for why.

Sonda A and Sonda B differ in exactly one thing.

Both run the identical policy: kill every known monster, explore every frontier, then head for the shrine. Loot is never a movement target for either one. Sonda A clears the floor picking up nothing. Sonda B clears the same floor, same seed, and keeps whatever it steps over along the way. Neither is the bot with options off — this is a frozen, deliberately dumb instrument, and it stays dumb even after the bot changes.

Both carry a 400-hp calibration hero so death never selects which samples survive — challenge and reward do not depend on the hero's hp, only on the damage the floor actually lands. Power and buffer are different: they are properties of the hero on arrival, so they come from a real ten-floor descent played by Sonda B alone, starting at the ordinary hero and carrying survivor bias same as before (measured at z = 4.89 for the modelled numbers — see curve-shape.md).
