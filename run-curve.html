What each floor asks of the hero who walks into it, in hp. A gauge, not a controller — nothing here adjusts itself. Tune generation on one side and hero accumulation on the other, and watch what the combination does to net challenge.

net challenge is the hp this floor is expected to cost, divided by the hp the hero arrived with. Above 1.00 the floor asks for more than the hero has. A healthy dungeon has this rising with depth; the shipped one falls, which is the whole problem.
