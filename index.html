Rogulidle

Ten floors, one bot. Every route is priced in hp, not steps: it gathers gear that pays for the walk, gives waking monsters a wide berth, picks the cheapest fight rather than the closest, and checks each step against the monsters' reply before taking it. Each floor's stairs are the last floor's shrine — what the hero carries down is everything the floors above handed over. A run ends at the tenth floor's shrine, or wherever the descent stops first.
—

Dimmed tiles are what the bot remembers, not what it can see — monsters there may have moved.
