I2 — clustering: lethality, not cost

Same roster, two spatial arrangements — spread (shipped generation, unchanged) against grouped (same monsters, gathered into clusters). Played by the REAL bot, starting at the ordinary 10-hp hero — this is a question about lethality and about the bot's own behaviour, not about the design in isolation, so no calibration tank here. See src/analysis/clustering.js.

Why the earlier "no effect" result is suspect.

That test used a 400-hp measuring hero. Spread or grouped, the hero meets the same creatures and takes a similar total number of blows — what changes is their concentration in time: three adjacent creatures strike the same turn. To 400 hp that is noise; to 10 hp it can be the whole game. This page asks about lethality (deaths, worst single-turn damage) and about whether the bot is un-grouping clusters back into sequential fights (fraction of turns with two or more monsters adjacent at once) instead of asking about cost.
